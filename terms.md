# Terms of Service — Carem Backup Rclone

**Last updated: October 24, 2026**

## Purpose

Carem Backup Rclone is a private utility intended for authorized backup and recovery operations for the Carem business management system.

## Authorized Use

Only authorized operators should configure the application, grant Google Drive access, manage encryption credentials, or perform backup and restoration operations.

## Google Drive Integration

The application uses Google Drive as a storage destination for encrypted backups. Use of Google Drive remains subject to Google's applicable terms and policies.

## Security Responsibilities

The operator is responsible for protecting OAuth credentials, encryption passphrases, and access to backup files.

Losing the encryption passphrase may make encrypted backups unrecoverable.

## Backup Availability

Backup completion and restoration must be verified. The utility does not guarantee uninterrupted availability of Google Drive, network connectivity, or the local computer.

These terms may be updated to reflect changes to the application's functionality or security practices.
